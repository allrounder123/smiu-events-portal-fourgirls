About the event

Explore the Future of Technology

The SMIU Technology Summit brings students together to explore emerging technologies, exchange ideas and learn about new developments in the digital world.

Students interested in software development, artificial intelligence, innovation and entrepreneurship can participate in discussions and interactive sessions.

  • 09:30 AM – Student Registration
  • 10:00 AM – Opening Ceremony
  • 11:00 AM – Technology Sessions
  • 12:00 PM – Interactive Activities
  • 01:00 PM – Closing Ceremony

Guest speakers, technology professionals and university faculty representatives will share their knowledge.

Confirmed speaker details will be announced by the event organizers.

  • Bring your valid university card.
  • Arrive at least 15 minutes early.
  • Respect speakers and participants.
  • Follow the instructions of organizers.
